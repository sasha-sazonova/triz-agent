01 / Purpose
02 / 13

From an engineering problem to a verified solution.

The agent accepts a task in natural language — RU or EN — and drives it along the TRIZ route: mini-problem → contradiction → IFR → principles and effects → concepts → independent critique → report with full traceability.

Who it is for

  • Engineers and R&D
  • Industrial safety
  • Patent research
  • Product teams

Input

  • System and constraints description
  • What to improve and what must not break
  • Success criterion and threat class

Output

  • Report 07 with traceability
  • Concepts with verdicts
  • Preliminary patent assessment
  • Engineering package: calculations, tests, suppliers
Compromise is capitulation. A solution separates the contradiction in space, time, or structure.
How the Heinrich agent
works
A copilot with gates: a human confirms the mini-problem, the contradiction, and the report. At most two questions — then assumptions, clearly marked.

03 / DEEP · ARIZ-85C
04 / 13

When LITE hits a wall, ARIZ-85C takes over.

Escalation triggers

01

Critique has rejected every concept twice.No PASS or CONSIDER — a different level of analysis is needed.

02

The parameter pair is an [approximation], and the first round produced no PASS or CONSIDER.First a full LITE round, then DEEP.

03

The user explicitly asks to go deeper.More resources, subsystems and fields.

04

The task shifts its framing: the mini- or macro-problem has to move.The system conflict cannot be resolved within the current frame.

Nine parts

1Problem analysis
2Resource model
3IFR and PC
4Resource mobilization
5Information fund
6Change or replace the problem
7Answer control
8Implementation
9Analysis of the solution process
DEEP is not “longer”. It is work with resources, subsystems and reframing — not a search through more ideas. After parts 1–4, a return to LITE on an enriched resource base is allowed.
Triggers are recorded in mode.md. DEEP — 40–90 minutes; parts up to 1200 seconds per subagent.

12 / Limits and Next Step
13 / 13

Method is stronger than an idea.

Heinrich does not promise patentability. It shows where the contradiction is, how to separate it, and what to verify next.

“Invention is the removal of a contradiction. Everything else is filing paperwork.”Heinrich · after G.S. Altshuller

What's next

  • Eval: 3 → 20–30 cases
  • Effects: 15 → 100+
  • EPO OPS / USPTO keys
  • Field DEEP
  • Delivery channel and background

Limits

  • Not a guarantee of patentability
  • Not a replacement for an engineer or a patent attorney
  • Without data — assumptions, not facts
  • DEEP has not been field-tested
  • Eval is still 3 seed cases
